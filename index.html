Post-COVID Disease Map

Many post-COVID patients have persistent symptoms without a mechanistic explanation. We integrate clinical, imaging, proteomic, immune and viral data into one open, layered disease map to uncover the underlying mechanisms.

What we do

From patient data to mechanistic insight in three steps.

1

Measure across scales

Clinical data and questionnaires, PET and MRI scans, proteomics, immune profiling and viral reactivation in 45 patients and 20 controls.

2

Build the map

Curated databases, literature and experimental and clinical data feed one layered disease map (system, tissue, molecular) in Minerva.

3

Derive insights

Pathway- and network-based analysis tests and improves the map and points to biomarker signatures for diagnosis and patient stratification.

Six systems, one disease
