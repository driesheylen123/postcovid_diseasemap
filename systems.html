The six systems

Each system is a section of the disease map.

Central nervous system In first version

Persistent neuroinflammation and disruption of the blood-brain barrier are central to our hypothesis. Brain networks (e.g. functional connectivity) are loaded into the map and can be overlaid with PET imaging data.

Immune system In first version

Covers the systemic immune response: immune cell composition and activation, viral persistence and epigenetic modifications in monocytes, with dysregulation by the autonomic and endocrine systems.

Gastrointestinal system Planned

Description to be added.

Musculoskeletal system Planned

Muscle-related mechanisms will be added after the first version of the map.

Vascular system In development

A blood coagulation layer (hemostasis, coagulation cascade, fibrinolysis) is already available in Minerva, with annotations and overlay data for individual elements. Further vascular detail will follow.

Cellular metabolism In first version

Metabolism is part of the first map version, with mitochondrial dysfunction to be added next. Metabolic activity is measured by whole-body [18F]FDG PET and proteomic panels on inflammation and metabolism.
